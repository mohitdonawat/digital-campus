## [Slide 1] Executive Overview: The Digital Faculty OS

### 1. The Real-College Problem
Faculty members in Indian technical universities face severe administrative burdens:
- Calling roll calls for 60 students per lecture wastes 15 minutes of every 50-minute teaching hour.
- Manually calculating monthly attendance percentages in thick paper register books.
- Signing paper registration forms, bonafide slips, and lab records by hand.
- Dealing with timetable clashes or absent colleagues without automated substitution tools.

### 2. The IES Faculty OS Solution
**Faculty OS** consolidates all administrative, teaching, and mentorship duties into a single mobile dashboard. Taking attendance takes under 20 seconds, timetable updates sync instantly, and students receive automated notifications without paperwork.

## [Slide 3] Student Account Verification & Identity Approval Gate

### 1. Step-by-Step Working Mechanism
- **Trigger**: New student registers on the app.
- **Faculty Alert**: The alert banner shows `X Students Pending Approval`.
- **Review Workflow**:
  - Professor taps "Review".
  - Inspects applicant credentials: Full Name, University Roll Number, RGPV Enrollment, Branch, Year, and Section.
- **Decision Actions**:
  - 🟢 **Approve (One-Tap)**: Updates database to `isApproved: true`. The student's dashboard unlocks immediately, and they are enrolled in the class attendance register and batch discussion group.
  - 🔴 **Reject**: Deletes bogus accounts, preventing outsiders from accessing college resources.

### 🗣️ Presenter Talking Points
> *"This gatekeeper mechanism prevents unauthorized users from accessing campus streams or student directories. Every student must be authenticated by their department faculty before their account is activated."*

## [Slide 4] One-Tap Classroom Attendance Register (20-Second Roll Call)

### 1. Exact Step-by-Step Operation
1. **Context Selection (5 seconds)**:
   - Professor selects Branch (`CSE`), Year (`3rd Year`), Semester (`5th Sem`), Section (`A`), Subject (`Operating Systems`), and Period (`Period 2`).
2. **Auto-Load Roster**:
   - The enrolled class roster loads in alphabetical order by Roll Number.
3. **⚡ The One-Tap Present Action (2 seconds)**:
   - Professor taps **"Mark All Present"**. All 60 student cards turn green (`PRESENT`) instantly.
4. **Marking Absentees (10 seconds)**:
   - Professor scans the classroom and taps only the 2 or 3 absent students, switching their cards to red (`ABSENT`).
5. **Submit & Instant Push (3 seconds)**:
   - Professor taps **"Submit Attendance"**.
   - Record writes to Firestore collection `attendance` with subject, period, timestamp, and student attendance map.
   - **Total Duration: Under 20 seconds.**
   - Student percentages update in real time on their personal meters.
