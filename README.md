## 🚀 4 Flagship AI Modules

### 1. 🎙️ AI Voice Assistant
- Interactive voice microphone with live animated audio soundwave visualizer.
- Speech recognition and synthesized voice response simulation.
- Real-time queries for attendance status, timetable substitute alerts, fee balances, bus routes, and exam predictions.
- Embedded actionable shortcuts within AI conversation bubbles.

### 2. 📈 Predictive Student Performance
- Multivariate regression model (R² = 0.91) trained on historic student cohorts, internal assessments, and attendance records.
- Predicts upcoming Semester SGPA with 95% confidence intervals.
- Subject-wise mastery radar highlighting critical risk subjects.
- **Interactive "What-If" Simulation Sandbox**: Adjust daily study hours and attendance targets to see real-time projected SGPA impact.

### 3. 🛡️ Early Dropout Prediction & Early Warning System (EWS)
- Multi-parameter risk algorithm tracking:
  1. Attendance slope (decay rate over 30 days)
  2. Academic backlogs and arrears
  3. Fee delinquency duration
  4. LMS & doubt session engagement index
- Categorizes student risk into Low, Moderate, and Critical danger tiers.
- **1-Tap Automated Counselor Interventions**: Auto-trigger parent WhatsApp alerts, schedule 1-on-1 mentor sessions, or assign remedial peer tutoring.

### 4. 🧠 Personalized Learning Recommendations
- Diagnostic skill-gap engine analyzing quiz errors and exam question responses.
- Tailored video playlists, visual cheatsheets, and adaptive practice quizzes.
- AI-generated **14-Day Exam Readiness Roadmap** with interactive milestone checkboxes.

## 🏛️ Core Integrated ERP Subsystems

1. **Student Lifecycle Management**: Tracks student journey from Central Admission & KYC to Core Specialization, Internships, Degree Conferral, and Alumni Network. Features a verifiable **Hanging PVC Lanyard ID Card** with barcode and digital seal.
2. **Smart Attendance Radar**: Real-time gauge for the statutory 75% RGPV/AICTE rule. Calculates exact "Safe Bunks" or "Consecutive Classes Needed", paired with geofenced anti-proxy check-in.
3. **Master Dynamic Timetable**: Weekly schedule with live faculty substitution notices and leave reasons.
4. **Digital Certificates & Attestation**: Instant Bonafide and Transcript generator sealed with cryptographic SHA-256 hashes and scannable QR verification proof.
5. **Student Fee Portal**: Transparent financial ledger, fee category breakdown, simulated instant payment gateway (UPI/Card), and downloadable official GST receipts.
6. **Hostel & Mess Management**: Room allotment details, 4-meal daily mess schedule, and digital E-Gate Pass workflow with warden digital approvals.
7. **Transport & Transit System**: Multi-route network, live bus GPS simulator with ETA countdown, speed tracker, driver hotline, and digital transit pass.
8. **Student Helpdesk & Anti-Ragging Cell**: 48-hour SLA grievance ticketing with AI automated routing and mandatory AICTE statutory safety escalation.
9. **Parent Portal**: Dedicated guardian view showing ward's live attendance, test scores, fee clearance, hostel logs, and direct faculty mentor calling hotline.
10. **Multi-Role Presentation Switcher**: Allows evaluators to switch between Student, Faculty, Admin, and Parent views in 1 click.
